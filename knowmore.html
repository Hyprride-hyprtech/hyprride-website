Every rental has two stories — the rider who wants the keys fast, and the shop that wants to run tight. Hyprverse is designed for both.

FOR RIDERS

Ride anywhere.

  • Book a bike in seconds — see live availability, real photos and the exact price before you tap.

  • Your ride history, receipts and rewards — all in one place.
FOR RENTAL SHOPS

Run the whole shop
from your pocket.

  • Every booking, every bike, every rupee — live on one dashboard, updated in real time.
  • Scan a licence, verify a rider and hand over keys in under two minutes.
  • Late returns chased automatically. Reminders go out so you don't have to.
  • Daily revenue, P&L and GST summaries that make your accountant smile.
  • Start with one counter, scale to a fleet across the city — same app, zero rework.
