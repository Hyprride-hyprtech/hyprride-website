    Ask us anything

    Replies from sunrise to midnight — usually in minutes.

    WhatsApp+91 7032887133
    01What documents are required to rent a bike?

    Your original Driving Licence and Aadhaar Card, shown at pickup. Verification is quick — message us on WhatsApp and we’ll guide you through it.

    02Is there a security deposit?

    Yes — a fully returnable deposit of ₹1,000 for local rides and ₹3,000 for outstation trips. It comes back in full when the bike is returned in good condition.

    03What’s included with every ride?

    A sanitised, fully serviced bike, a certified helmet, and on-ride WhatsApp support — all within the listed price. No hidden extras.

    04Is there a kilometre limit?

    Each slab includes a distance allowance: 1 hr · 15 km, 3 hrs · 40 km, 5 hrs · 60 km, 7 hrs · 80 km, 12 hrs · 100 km and 24 hrs · 120 km. Riding beyond that is charged extra. Planning a longer trip? Ask us about Unlimited KM on WhatsApp.

    05What is the fuel policy?

    Bikes are handed over with enough fuel to reach the nearest pump. Fuel during the ride is on the rider — just return it with a similar level.

    06Can I extend my ride? What about late returns?

    Yes — message us on WhatsApp before your time ends and we’ll extend the booking if the bike is available. Unannounced late returns are billed hourly: ₹93/hr for 110cc, ₹116/hr for 125cc and ₹152/hr for 160cc.
