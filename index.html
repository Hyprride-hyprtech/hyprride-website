FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before you ride. Still unsure? We're a message away.

What documents are required to rent a bike?

A valid driving licence and a government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, passport or similar). Verification is quick and done at pickup — message us on WhatsApp and we'll guide you through it.

Is there a security deposit?

A small refundable deposit may apply depending on the vehicle. It's returned in full when the bike comes back in good condition — no deductions, no drama.

What's included with every ride?

A sanitised, fully serviced bike, a certified helmet, and on-ride WhatsApp support — all within the listed price. No hidden extras.

What is the fuel policy?

Bikes are handed over with enough fuel to reach the nearest pump. Fuel during the ride is on the rider — just return it with a similar level.

Is there a kilometre limit?

Our hourly and daily plans come with generous limits for city riding. Planning a longer trip? Message us and we'll set you up with the right plan.

Can I extend my ride?

Yes — just message us on WhatsApp before your time ends and we'll extend the booking if the bike is available. Support is one message away.
