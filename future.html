One app, two sides

Built for both sidesof the counter.

Every rental has two stories — the rider who wants the keys fast, and the shop that wants to run tight. Hyprverse is designed for both.

For riders

Tap. Verify once. Ride anywhere.

  • Book a bike in seconds — see live availability, real photos and the exact price before you tap.
  • Do your KYC once, ride everywhere — your verified profile works across every shop on the network.
  • Extend your ride from your phone. No calls, no renegotiation, no stress.
  • Every charge itemised. If it’s not on the screen, you don’t pay it.
  • Your ride history, receipts and rewards — all in one place.
For rental shops

Run the whole shop from your pocket.

  • Every booking, every bike, every rupee — live on one dashboard, updated in real time.
  • Scan a licence, verify a rider and hand over keys in under two minutes.
  • Late returns chased automatically. Reminders go out so you don’t have to.
  • Daily revenue, P&L and GST summaries that make your accountant smile.
  • Start with one counter, scale to a fleet across the city — same app, zero rework.

Hyprverse for shops

The future of rental shopsis digital-first.

We’re building Hyprverse — a complete management platform designed from the ground up for small and growing vehicle rental businesses across India.

Today, most rental shops run on WhatsApp threads, paper registers and gut feeling. Hyprverse replaces all of that with a clean, fast, mobile-ready app that just works.

  • Bookings at your fingertips

    Walk-ins, advances, extensions — every hire tracked in one place. No more scattered chats or missed handovers.

  • Know your customer instantly

    Aadhaar and licence scanned at pickup. Verified details, trusted history — before the keys leave the counter.

  • Revenue, not guesswork

    Live revenue, P&L, GST reports and daily summaries — so you always know where your business stands.

  • Cross-shop safety network

    Flagged or blacklisted riders are surfaced across the network — protecting every shop, including yours.

  • Run your team with roles

    Owner, manager, staff — everyone sees what they should, and nothing they shouldn’t. No accidental edits.

  • Pricing that makes sense

    Flexible slab pricing per category, automatic late fees, deposit handling and GST — done correctly, every time.

  • From one shop to a network.

    Hyprverse is designed to grow with you — from a single counter to a multi-city fleet. The infrastructure is already being built for it.
